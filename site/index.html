Longhand

Transcripts that never leave your device

Longhand records or imports a conversation and turns it into a transcript, with each person's words attributed to them. Speech recognition and speaker detection run on your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch or Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Speaker-attributed transcripts, with names you can correct and voices it can learn to recognise.
  • Many languages, including Hebrew and Russian, and recordings that switch between them.
  • Record on the phone, the iPad, the Mac or the wrist; share recordings in from other apps.
  • Edits, markers, and exports to Markdown, plain text and JSON.
